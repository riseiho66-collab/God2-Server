executes the source qualification/publication and rechecks identity again. Only
after that game publication does IO locate the exact held frame, recheck current
native world-tail/identity/battle boundary, release it and resume a stopped writer.

change is to stop at an empty/held head; older active writes may finish and later
ordinary output cannot pass the held native record. Multiple queued held frames
keep their existing order. False/throw/stale/close/encoding/capacity failure closes
this connection and refuses held bytes rather than inventing success or rolling
back already committed owner/storage state.
