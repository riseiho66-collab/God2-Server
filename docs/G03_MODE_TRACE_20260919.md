# G03 Mode-Aware Qualification Review

Decision: **NEED_MORE_EVIDENCE**. G02 remains unchanged. G04 is deferred.
These are qualification and focused-return experiments, not newly certified
G03 captures. No new G02 or G03 certificate was issued for these runs.

## Directly Observed Completion

Two fresh runs (`exact-ret-a`, `exact-ret-b`) observed native byte C3 at
0x0054ED7A, decoded as RET. Before the instruction, ESP was 0x001AFEFC and
the stack return address was 0x0054A46E. One native debugger step reached
0x0054A46E with ESP 0x001AFF00 in the same PID/TID.

| Run | PID | TID | Outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| exact-ret-a | 0x116C | 0xA6C | RETURNS |
| exact-ret-b | 0x4E98 | 0x627C | RETURNS |

This establishes the exact final control transfer, not a complete trace of
the third routine. The interval from 0x5479BD to the RET remains partial.
Result registers and native stack output remain in the raw logs.

## Method Qualification

`mode-qual` matched one WoW64 boundary: entry 0x77547000, ESP 0x1AFE68,
expected return 0x775CA03C; same-thread return had ESP 0x1AFE6C.
Subsequent stepping reached KERNELBASE!GetFileType+0x8C.
This is a limited boundary pass, not a qualified startup tracing method.

The extended software-return experiment (`mode-a`) matched three boundaries,
then failed at index 3. Expected return 0x775C9D0C with ESP 0x1AF9DC was
observed with ESP 0x1AFBA0 instead. Matching PC alone is not a matched return.

`mode-hw-a` contained invalid breakpoint syntax and is retained as TOOL_ERROR.
The corrected `mode-hw-b` matched three boundaries, then stopped at
0x77547000 / ESP 0x1AFE64 instead of the expected return. The cause is not
established; nested callbacks or unwinding are not asserted as explanations.
The prepared `mode-b` script was not executed.

These failures leave a material coverage gap. Two exact-RET reproductions
do not substitute for the requested two complete startup reproductions
after trace-method qualification.

## Evidence Scope

Authoritative derived output: `artifacts/reports/g03-mode-20260919-v2/`.
The first report directory is retained but superseded: its branch ledger
treated the next recorded PC as an immediate successor. Version 2 leaves
branch target/taken unknown where continuous coverage is unproved.

Native decode, Capstone boundaries and mapped bytes agreed for all compared
rows: mode-a 176, mode-hw-a 36, mode-hw-b 176, exact-ret-a 7, exact-ret-b 7.
The invalid-tool run's byte agreement does not qualify its tracing method.
There are no comparable mapped rows for mode-qual.

Three 4-byte hardware write watches covered [0x54A3E8,0x54A3EC),
[0x54EBC0,0x54EBC4), and [0x54ED78,0x54ED7C). No hit markers were observed.
This is only 12 bytes, not a full executable-region mutation detector, and
does not exclude secondary unpacking or transient writes elsewhere.
Watches were cleared before the final single-step RET.

Runtime page-protection snapshots exist at selected stops. The full requested
protection timeline is incomplete. DEP policy was NOT queried for these PIDs;
no DEP result from an older PID is reused. Original PE section flags remain
unchanged. PE flags, runtime protection, and process DEP policy are separate.

## Acceptance Questions

1. Candidate reproduced: yes, in focused experiments.
2. Coherent startup continuation: endpoints yes; full causal slice incomplete.
3. Exact third-routine completion: yes, RET directly observed twice.
4. Material gaps closed: no, extended boundary qualification failed.
5. Remaining gaps proven non-material: no.
6. Secondary unpack absent: not established by the limited write watches.
7. No packer return: not established over the incomplete startup window.
8. Static decoding agrees: yes for the recorded and compared rows only.
9. Reproducible: exact RET yes; qualified full startup no.
10. Counter-evidence: no established contradiction to candidate identity;
    tracing mismatches invalidate a complete-coverage claim.

Call/branch ledgers and coverage maps are partial research outputs, not a
complete implementation of all requested G03 acceptance evidence. In
particular, complete nested calls, flags, protection stages, and critical
mutation coverage remain open.

Next work must qualify a method that explains or avoids the fourth-boundary
mismatch, potentially a mode-aware debugger context method after tooling
inventory. Then perform new formal captures with their own G02 certificates.
Do not enlarge instruction counts as a substitute for mode visibility.
